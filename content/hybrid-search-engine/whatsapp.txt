*AI Frontiers Architecture Lab*
Hybrid Search Engine

Combine keyword and vector retrieval.

Takeaway: Hybrid search is not just about adding vector search to keyword search; it's about intelligently fusing their strengths. Score Fusion provides a robust baseline for combining lexical and semantic signals, while Diversity Control (MMR) is essential to prevent the 'echo chamber' effect of redundant results. This architecture offers the best balance of accuracy and performance for enterprise applications.
