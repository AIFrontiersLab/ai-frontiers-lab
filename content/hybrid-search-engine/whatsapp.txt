*AI Frontiers Architecture Lab*
Hybrid Search Engine

Combine keyword and vector retrieval.

Takeaway: Hybrid search is not a silver bullet; it is a balancing act. The critical engineering insight is that fusion without diversity control leads to information collapse. MMR is the necessary third component to make hybrid retrieval truly effective and Z-score normalization ensures robust score fusion across different retriever distributions.
