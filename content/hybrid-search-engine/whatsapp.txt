*AI Frontiers Architecture Lab*
Hybrid Search Engine

Combine keyword and vector retrieval.

Takeaway: Hybrid search is not merely a feature; it is a necessity for robust enterprise retrieval. Lexical matching ensures precision for exact terms and IDs, while dense retrieval captures semantic intent. By using Reciprocal Rank Fusion (RRF), we combine these strengths without the fragility of score normalization. The architecture prioritizes resilience (graceful degradation) and observability (strict versioning), ensuring that the system remains reliable even when individual components fail or drift.
